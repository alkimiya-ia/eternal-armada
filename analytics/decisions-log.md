# Decisions Log

This file records durable project decisions so they are not lost across conversations.

## 2026-10

### Release planning
- La Última Guardia premiere: **21 October 2026 at 18:30 CEST**.
- Picas en la Niebla follows La Última Guardia and is planned as the main November release.
- Bajo tu Manto is reserved for **8 December 2026**, tied to Empel.

### Flanders narrative
- Picas en la Niebla is the direct thematic continuation of El Camino Español.
- El Camino Español tells the journey to Flanders; Picas en la Niebla tells what waits at the end of that road.
- Plan a dedicated Flanders/Tercios playlist once Picas is live.

### Analytics interpretation
- El Camino Español is treated primarily as an evergreen acquisition/discovery asset.
- Do not alter its current title/thumbnail while long-tail Suggested traffic remains healthy.
- Use Picas en la Niebla as the first deliberate test of transferring El Camino Español traffic into a narrative sequel.
- Mediohombre remains a strong candidate for internal recommendations because of its historically strong CTR/retention/conversion behavior.

### Cross-channel policy
- Keep Lyravolt separate from Eternal Armada. Lyravolt is optimized for long listening sessions (relaxation, focus, gym, driving), so do not use its end screen to push Eternal Armada.
- Do not cross-promote Eternal Armada into Morrowveil, Fault Meridian or Al-Kimiya by default because the listening intent and style differ.

### Distribution
- DistroKid is now part of the release workflow.
- Distributor track titles should remain clean, without parenthetical genre keywords.
- Current genre convention: Metal / Rock español.

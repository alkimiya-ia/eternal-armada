# Video Intro Pattern

Use short historical framing before the song when the story benefits from context.

Recommended structure:

1. Place + date.
2. Immediate historical situation.
3. Human or dramatic stake.
4. Final line that connects directly to the song title.

Examples used / planned:
- Atado a los suyos: Cascorro, Cuba, 1896.
- Cicatrices de Lepanto Vol. I: Gulf of Lepanto, 7 October 1571.
- La Última Guardia: Baler, Philippines, 1898.

Keep intros concise, cinematic and readable before the music fully opens.

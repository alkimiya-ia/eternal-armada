# Visual Style

## Core identity

- Cinematic historical realism.
- Serious, mature tone.
- Weathered materials, smoke, mist, rain, sea spray and natural light where appropriate.
- Avoid overt fantasy unless intentionally stylized for a specific release.
- Maintain a consistent Eternal Armada wordmark treatment across release artwork.
- Square distributor covers: 3000 × 3000 px.
- YouTube thumbnails: 16:9, composition optimized for small-screen legibility.

## Typography

For lyric videos and historical titles, use a classical serif family similar to the Cinzel style used in prior videos.

General hierarchy:
- Song / release title: strong serif display.
- Eternal Armada: smaller but clearly visible.
- Avoid excessive copy on thumbnails.

## Motion

For image-to-video animation:
- Prefer subtle parallax, slow push-ins, environmental movement and restrained character motion.
- Preserve faces, flags, uniforms and historical architecture.
- Avoid over-animation that makes still artwork feel synthetic.

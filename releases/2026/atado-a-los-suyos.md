# Atado a los suyos

## Release

- Historical subject: Eloy Gonzalo at Cascorro, Cuba, 1896.
- Premiere: **30 September 2026**

## YouTube positioning

**Atado a los suyos – Eloy Gonzalo | Metal histórico en español**

## Story hook

Before leaving under enemy fire, Eloy Gonzalo asked to be tied with a rope so his comrades could recover his body if he did not return.

This gesture is the central narrative image of the song and launch campaign.

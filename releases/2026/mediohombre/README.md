# Mediohombre

## Historical subject

Blas de Lezo and the defense of Cartagena de Indias.

## YouTube positioning

Current title convention:

**Mediohombre – Blas de Lezo | Metal histórico en español**

## Strategic role

Mediohombre is one of the strongest Eternal Armada videos for retention and conversion behavior.

Historically observed strengths:
- Very strong click-through rate.
- Strong average percentage viewed.
- Good subscriber conversion relative to channel size.
- Good returning-viewer behavior.

Use it as a strong internal recommendation candidate when thematic fit is acceptable.

## Visual identity

Blas de Lezo should be immediately recognizable as the historical anchor in thumbnail and artwork.

The preferred visual tone is cinematic historical realism, with Cartagena / naval context, restrained gold typography and the Eternal Armada brand.

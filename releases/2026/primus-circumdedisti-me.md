# Primus Circumdedisti Me

## Release

- Historical subject: Juan Sebastián Elcano and the first circumnavigation.
- Premiere: **8 September 2026**
- Date intentionally tied to the arrival of the Nao Victoria at Seville on 8 September 1522.

## YouTube positioning

Primary title:
**Primus Circumdedisti Me – Juan Sebastián Elcano | Metal histórico en español**

A future title test may place the historical name first if CTR optimization is needed:
**Juan Sebastián Elcano – Primus Circumdedisti Me | Metal histórico en español**

## Strategic notes

The video received broad cold-audience exposure early, but CTR was weaker than the strongest catalog performers.

Historical snapshot used in planning:
- 269 views in the early launch window.
- 2,438 impressions in the first four days.
- 1.29% CTR in that period.
- Around 44% retention at 30 seconds.

Treat these values as historical snapshots, not current lifetime metrics.

## Core hook

“Cinco naves partieron. Una sola regresó.”

Use the Nao Victoria and Elcano as the primary visual and historical anchors.

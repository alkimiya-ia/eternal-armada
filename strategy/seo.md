# SEO and Metadata

## Standard YouTube title format

`<Song title> – <Historical figure/event> | Metal histórico en español`

Use the historical figure or event as the recognizable search anchor.

Examples:
- Mediohombre – Blas de Lezo | Metal histórico en español
- Primus Circumdedisti Me – Juan Sebastián Elcano | Metal histórico en español
- La Última Guardia – Los Últimos de Filipinas | Metal histórico en español

## Metadata rule

Do not add genre descriptors such as `(Historic Metal)` to official distributor track titles. Keep distributor metadata clean and use platform genre fields for classification.

## DistroKid genre convention

- Primary: Metal
- Secondary: Rock español

Maintain consistency across releases unless a specific release clearly requires another classification.

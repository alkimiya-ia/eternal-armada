# YouTube Strategy

## Channel positioning

Eternal Armada focuses on Spanish historical heavy/power metal, combining historical subjects, cinematic visuals, lyric-driven videos and long-form song releases.

## Current strategic pillars

- Use recognizable historical figures, campaigns and battles in titles and thumbnails.
- Keep titles clear: `Song title – Historical figure/event | Metal histórico en español`.
- Use Community posts to build narrative before premieres instead of only posting reminders.
- Treat playlists as a major watch-time tool.
- Use end screens to move viewers into related songs or thematic playlists.
- Use Shorts mainly as 15–30 second high-energy discovery hooks, not as the primary conversion mechanism.
- Preserve strong visual consistency across covers and thumbnails.

## Acquisition vs conversion

### El Camino Español
Treat as an evergreen acquisition asset:
- Strong long-tail Suggested traffic.
- Broad new-viewer discovery.
- Good CTR at scale.
- Strong playlist behavior.

Do not change its title or thumbnail while this behavior remains healthy.

### Mediohombre
Treat as a strong conversion / retention asset:
- Historically strong CTR.
- Strong retention.
- Good subscriber and returning-viewer behavior.

## Flanders arc

Build an explicit narrative path:

1. El Camino Español – the route to Flanders.
2. Picas en la Niebla – what waits at the end of the road.
3. Potential future continuation: Breda or another Flanders campaign story.

When Picas is published:
- End screen from El Camino Español to Picas.
- End screen from Picas to either El Camino Español or the Flanders playlist.
- Add sequential playlist: `Los Tercios y la Guerra de Flandes | Eternal Armada`.
- Add pinned-comment and description cross-links.
